External release for shop shutters

External release box for shop shutters in Barcelona

We replace and install release boxes for the motor of your business's metal shutter, also known in Spain as cofres de desbloqueo, cofres blindados, cajetines or blindinos. With one, you can open your premises even during a power cut or if the motor fails.

What it is

What is a release box and what is it for?

Metal shutter motors have an electromagnetic brake that locks the axle when the motor is stopped. That way the shutter can't drop on its own or be forced up from outside. The problem comes when there's a power cut or the motor breaks down: with the brake on, the shutter won't move.

The release box is a small lockable box fitted to the façade next to the shutter. Inside there's a lever or pull handle connected to the motor's brake by a steel cable. When you operate it, the brake is released and the shutter can be raised by hand. It's essential when the shutter is the only way into the premises.

Depending on the manufacturer or installer, it goes by several names in Spain: caja de desbloqueo para motor de persiana, cajetín de desbloqueo exterior, cofre de desbloqueo, cofre blindado con desbloqueo, taquilla de desbloqueo or blindino. They are all the same thing, and we replace and install all of them.

How it works

How to open the shutter without power

You open the box with its key and pull the lever: the cable disengages the motor's brake and the shutter is free to be raised by hand. When the power comes back, you return the lever to its position, the brake locks the axle again and the motor works as usual.

On large, heavy shutters it takes some strength, because you're lifting the shutter's full weight without help from the motor. That's why the cable and brake need to be properly adjusted.

  • Lever or pull handle connected to the release cable.
  • Euro cylinder or tubular key lock.
  • On some models, an up/down switch.
  • Surface-mounted or recessed into the façade.

Replacement

When does the release box need replacing?

Being out on the street, the box has to withstand rain, sun, salt air and sometimes vandalism. These are the situations we're called out for most often:

Forced or vandalised lock

A smashed cylinder, a bent cover or the lock filled with silicone or glue. We replace the box or just the cylinder, depending on the damage.

Seized or rusty cylinder

The key won't go in or won't turn because of damp and dirt. If the box is fine, we replace the cylinder; if it's corroded, the whole box.

The lever doesn't release the motor

You pull the lever and the shutter is still braked. It's usually the release cable: slack, unhooked or snapped. We check it, adjust it or replace it all the way to the motor.

Broken box or cover that won't close

Old plastic boxes crack in the sun and leave the mechanism exposed. We replace them with tougher aluminium or steel boxes.

You've lost the key

Without the key to the box, you won't be able to open your premises during a power cut. We open it and replace the cylinder so you have your own keys again.

Your shutter has no external release

If the motor can only be released from inside and the shutter is the only way in, a power cut leaves you locked out. We install a release box on the façade.

Security

Is it safe to have the release on the street?

Yes, if it's the right box and it's properly installed. The box protects the lever and the cable: without its key, the motor's brake can't be released. To keep it that way, it's best to:

  • Choose an armoured metal box if it's very exposed.
  • Use a security cylinder that resists drilling and bumping.
  • Fix the box with screws that can't be reached from outside.
  • Not keep the box key inside the premises: in a power cut you couldn't get to it.
  • Combine it with a good lock or padlock on the shutter.

Price

How much does it cost to replace a release box?

It depends on what needs doing: replacing just the cylinder isn't the same as fitting a new box and running a cable from the motor. We give you a fixed quote before we start. The main factors are:

  • The box model: plastic, aluminium or steel, with or without a switch.
  • Whether the release cable can be reused or needs replacing.
  • Whether the box is surface-mounted or recessed into the façade.
  • The type of lock: standard, high-security or keyed alike with other keys.
  • The condition of the motor and the electromagnetic brake.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions about release boxes

Are a caja de desbloqueo, a cofre blindado and a blindino the same thing?

Yes. Caja de desbloqueo, cajetín de desbloqueo exterior, cofre de desbloqueo, cofre blindado, taquilla de desbloqueo and blindino are different names for the same thing: a lockable box that releases the shutter motor's brake from outside.

How do I open my shop shutter if there's a power cut?

Open the release box with its key, pull the lever and raise the shutter by hand. With the brake released, nothing holds the shutter in place, so don't let go of it halfway. When the power comes back, return the lever to its position so the motor works normally again.

I pull the lever and the shutter doesn't release. What's wrong?

Most often the release cable has gone slack, come unhooked or snapped, although the motor's brake can also fail. Don't force the shutter: we'll check the cable and the motor and leave everything properly adjusted.

Can you replace just the lock on the box?

Yes. We are locksmiths, so if the box is in good condition we replace just the cylinder, with a standard or security one. If the box is broken or rusty, it's best to replace the whole thing.

My shutter has no external release. Can one be installed?

In most cases, yes, as long as the motor has an electromagnetic brake with cable release, which is common on commercial metal shutters. We check it and, if needed, replace the cable so it reaches the box.

I've lost the key to my release box. What should I do?

Call us. We open the box and replace the cylinder so you have new keys. In the meantime, if there's a power cut you won't be able to release the shutter from outside, so it's best not to put it off.
