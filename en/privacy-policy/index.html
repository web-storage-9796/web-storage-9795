Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Basic data protection information

  • Controller: Cerrajería Merafex, S.L. (Tax ID B88741087).
  • Purposes: to answer your enquiries and quote requests, to provide and invoice the services you hire, to protect the website from spam and to measure how well our Google Ads advertising works.
  • Legal basis: pre-contractual steps and performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interest and, for advertising cookies, your consent.
  • Recipients: we do not share your data with third parties unless required by law. We use technology providers, some of them located outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards required by the GDPR.
  • Rights: access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability and withdrawal of consent, by writing to info@merafex.es. You may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency.
  • Further information: set out below.

This privacy policy explains how Cerrajería Merafex, S.L. processes the personal data it collects through the website persianas.merafex.es and the contact channels shown on it (telephone, WhatsApp and email), in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 of 5 December on the Protection of Personal Data and Guarantee of Digital Rights (LOPDGDD), and Spanish Law 34/2002 of 11 July on Information Society Services and Electronic Commerce (LSSI-CE).

This English version is provided for information. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Data controller

  • Company: Cerrajería Merafex, S.L.
  • Tax ID (NIF): B88741087
  • Address: Pl. de la Universitat, 3, 08007 Barcelona, Spain
  • Telephone: +34 611 37 54 95
  • Email: info@merafex.es

Cerrajería Merafex, S.L. is not required to appoint a Data Protection Officer, as its activity does not fall within the cases set out in Article 37 GDPR or Article 34 LOPDGDD. For any question about the processing of your data, you can write to info@merafex.es or to the postal address above.

2. Personal data we process

2.1. Data you give us

  • Contact form: name, telephone number, email address, postcode (optional) and the content of your message.
  • Telephone, WhatsApp and email: name, telephone number or email address, the content of the conversation and, where applicable, any photos or videos of the shutter you send us so that we can assess the job.
  • Provision of the service: address of the property where the work is carried out, description of the fault or installation, accepted quotes, invoicing details (name or company name, tax ID and address) and the details needed to process payment (for example, the reference of a bank transfer or a Bizum payment).

We do not ask for special categories of data (health, beliefs, ethnic origin, etc.). Please do not include them in your messages. If you give us data about other people (for example, the owner or tenant of the property, or the building's property manager), you confirm that you have informed them and are authorised to pass their data on to us.

2.2. Data obtained when you browse the website

  • Technical connection data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, pages requested and date and time of access. This data is processed by the providers that host the website and serve its resources, and is needed to show you the page and protect it from abuse.
  • Anti-spam check: the contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile, which analyses technical signals from the browser and device to tell people apart from automated programs.
  • Advertising measurement: the website includes the Google Ads tag with Google's consent mode. It records as a "conversion" the submission of the form and clicks on the call, WhatsApp or email buttons, so that we know whether our ads generate enquiries. If you accept advertising cookies, Google uses cookies to link your visit to the ad you clicked and to personalise advertising. If you do not accept them, the tag does not set or read cookies on your device, but it does send Google cookieless signals (the type of event and technical connection data such as your IP address and browser), which Google uses in aggregate to estimate conversions.

What you type in the form is not sent to Google. Details of cookies and similar technologies are set out in the Cookie policy.

2.3. Data that does not come from you

On the website we show reviews that their authors have published on our Google profile. We reproduce the author's public name, the rating, the text and the date as they appear on Google.

3. Purposes, legal bases and retention periods

For each purpose we state the legal basis for the processing under Article 6(1) GDPR and how long we keep the data. Once these periods have passed, the data is deleted or, where the law so requires, blocked and made available only to courts and public authorities for the limitation period of any possible liability (Article 32 LOPDGDD).

3.1. Answering enquiries and quote requests

  • Purpose: to reply to the messages you send us through the form, by telephone, WhatsApp or email, and to prepare quotes.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Where the enquiry is not related to hiring a service, our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry. If you do not go on to hire the service, we delete the data within 12 months at the latest.

3.2. Providing the services you hire

  • Purpose: to organise and carry out the work, communicate with you, process payment and handle guarantees, incidents and complaints.
  • Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and compliance with obligations under consumer protection law (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Retention: for the duration of the relationship and afterwards for the guarantee period and the limitation period for claims arising from the contract (generally 5 years under Article 1964 of the Spanish Civil Code).

3.3. Invoicing and accounting and tax obligations

  • Purpose: to issue invoices and keep the accounts.
  • Legal basis: compliance with legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR), in particular tax and commercial law.
  • Retention: 6 years from the last accounting entry (Article 30 of the Spanish Commercial Code), a period that also covers the 4-year tax limitation period (Article 66 of Spanish General Tax Law 58/2003).

3.4. Protecting the website and the form from spam

  • Purpose: to serve the website securely and prevent automated form submissions.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in ensuring the security and proper functioning of the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: Merafex does not store this data. The hosting and verification providers keep it for the limited periods stated in their policies.

3.5. Measuring and personalising our advertising

  • Purpose: to find out whether our Google Ads campaigns generate enquiries and, if you agree, to show you personalised advertising.
  • Legal basis: the use of advertising cookies and ad personalisation are based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 22(2) LSSI-CE), which you may withdraw at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. The cookieless signals sent when you do not accept are based on our legitimate interest in measuring the effectiveness of our advertising in aggregate (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), to which you may object.
  • Retention: Google Ads cookies last up to 90 days, as set out in the Cookie policy. Your cookie choice is stored for 12 months. Google keeps the data it receives in accordance with its own policy.

3.6. Showing customer reviews

  • Purpose: to show genuine opinions about our services on the website.
  • Legal basis: legitimate interest in sharing our customers' feedback, based on reviews that their authors have made public on Google (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: for as long as the review remains published on Google or until its author objects. If you ask us to, we will remove it from the website.

3.7. Marketing communications

Data from the form and our other contact channels is not used to send you advertising. We will only send you marketing communications by electronic means if you have given us your express and specific consent to do so (separate from reading this policy) or if you are a customer and the communication concerns services similar to those you hired, in accordance with Article 21 LSSI-CE. In both cases you may object at any time, easily and free of charge, from the communication itself or by writing to info@merafex.es. We will keep your data for this purpose until you withdraw your consent or object.

4. Whether providing data is mandatory

The form fields marked with an asterisk (*) are needed to deal with your request. If you do not provide them, we will not be able to reply to you through that channel. To provide and invoice a service we also need the address of the job and invoicing details, which are required by law.

5. Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing of your data, including profiling, that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you. The anti-spam check on the form is an automated technical check. If it prevents you from sending the form, you can contact us by telephone, WhatsApp or email. If you accept advertising cookies, Google may use the data to personalise the ads it shows you. You can manage this in Google My Ad Center.

6. Recipients of the data

6.1. Disclosure to third parties

We do not sell or share your data. We will only disclose it to third parties where necessary or where there is a legal obligation:

  • Public authorities, courts and law enforcement agencies: where required by law or requested in the exercise of their functions (for example, the Spanish Tax Agency).
  • Banks and payment providers: to process card, Bizum or bank transfer payments.
  • Manufacturers and distributors: only where the service requires it, with the minimum data necessary (for example, to process the guarantee for a motor or another installed component).

6.2. Providers that process data on behalf of Merafex

To provide our services we use providers that access personal data as processors, with whom we have accepted the data processing terms required by Article 28 GDPR:

  • GitHub, Inc. (USA): website hosting (GitHub Pages).
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co): forwarding contact form messages to our email.
  • Google (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC): email and Google Ads conversion measurement.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA): anti-spam check on the form (Cloudflare Turnstile).
  • Accounting and tax advisers, where applicable, to manage invoicing and taxes.

6.3. Third-party services you choose to use

  • Google Ads (Google Ireland Limited): if you accept advertising cookies, Google also processes the data as an independent controller to personalise ads, in accordance with its privacy policy. Find out more about how Google uses data from its partners' websites.
  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Limited): if you message us on WhatsApp, the platform processes your data as an independent controller under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Maps (Google Ireland Limited): the service area map is embedded from Google, which receives your IP address and your browser's technical data when it loads.
  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited): the website only links to our profiles. If you interact with them, Meta processes your data under its own privacy policy.

7. International transfers

Some of the providers listed (such as GitHub, Google, Cloudflare or Meta) are established in the United States or belong to groups headquartered there, so your data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. These transfers rely on Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795 of 10 July 2023 on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for entities certified under that framework. In other cases they rely on the standard contractual clauses approved by Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914 (Articles 45 and 46 GDPR). You can request more information about these safeguards by writing to info@merafex.es.

8. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights, recognised in Articles 15 to 22 GDPR, at any time:

  • Access: to find out whether we process your data and obtain a copy.
  • Rectification: to correct inaccurate data or complete incomplete data.
  • Erasure: to ask us to delete your data where, among other reasons, it is no longer needed.
  • Restriction: to ask us to suspend processing in the cases provided for in the GDPR.
  • Portability: to receive the data you gave us in a structured, commonly used format, where processing is based on your consent or on a contract and is carried out by automated means.
  • Objection: to object to processing based on our legitimate interest, on grounds relating to your particular situation, and in any case to marketing communications.
  • Not to be subject to automated individual decisions, including profiling.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. You can also reject advertising cookies using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

How to exercise your rights

  • Email: info@merafex.es, with "Data protection" in the subject line.
  • Post: Cerrajería Merafex, S.L., Pl. de la Universitat, 3, 08007 Barcelona, Spain.

Please state the right you wish to exercise and how we can reply to you. Exercising your rights is free of charge. We will reply within one month of receiving your request. This period may be extended by two further months for complex or numerous requests, in which case we will tell you about the extension within the first month (Article 12 GDPR). We will only ask for additional information to confirm your identity where we have reasonable doubts about it. You may exercise your rights through a representative, providing proof of representation. Template request forms are available on the Spanish Data Protection Agency's website.

People linked to a deceased person for family or similar reasons, as well as their heirs, may request access to, rectification or erasure of that person's data under the terms of Article 3 LOPDGDD.

9. Complaints to the supervisory authority

If you believe that we have not handled your rights properly or that the processing of your data does not comply with the law, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). We recommend that you contact us first so that we can try to resolve it.

  • Website: www.aepd.es
  • Electronic office: sedeagpd.gob.es
  • Address: C/ Jorge Juan, 6, 28001 Madrid, Spain
  • Telephone: +34 901 100 099 / +34 912 663 517

10. Security measures

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk to protect your data against loss, alteration, unauthorised access or processing (Article 32 GDPR), including HTTPS encryption of communications with the website, access to data limited to the people who need it for their work, and the choice of providers offering sufficient guarantees. In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the AEPD within 72 hours at the latest and, where it is likely to result in a high risk to your rights, we will also notify you (Articles 33 and 34 GDPR).

11. Minors

Our services may only be hired by adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 14, who may only provide it with the consent of their parents or guardians (Article 7 LOPDGDD). If we find that a child under 14 has given us data without that consent, we will delete it.

12. Accuracy of data

You confirm that the data you give us is true, accurate and up to date, and you agree to tell us about any changes.

13. Changes to this policy

We may amend this privacy policy to reflect changes in the law or in our processing. The date of the last update appears at the top of the document. If the changes are significant, we will say so clearly on the website and, where processing is based on your consent, we will ask for it again.
