Repair

Blind repair

We diagnose the fault and fix it the same day whenever possible: straps, winders, slats, shafts, brackets and motors.

  • Stuck or misaligned blind
  • Broken strap or damaged winder
  • Damaged or misaligned slats
  • Blind that will not go up or down
  • Shaft or bracket problems
  • Faulty motor or motorised blind

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does blind repair cost?

It depends on the type of fault and the blind. That is why we always visit or assess the case first and give you a fixed, no-obligation quote.

Can you repair every type of blind?

We work with aluminium, PVC, security, roller and metal blinds, both manual and motorised. Tell us about your case and we will advise you.

Can an existing blind be motorised?

In most cases, yes. We assess the condition of the housing and shaft to fit a suitable motor without replacing the entire blind.

How long does an installation take?

A standard installation is usually completed in one visit. For larger projects or several units, we provide an estimated timeframe in the quote.

Do you work with aluminium and PVC blinds?

Yes, we install and repair both materials, as well as security and metal blinds.

Do you install blinds in commercial premises?

Yes, we work in homes, commercial premises, shops and offices, including security blinds and metal shutters.

Can I control my blind from my phone?

It is possible with installations compatible with app-based remote control. We will tell you which options work for your blind and motor.

Does the visit have a cost?

The initial assessment and quote are free and without obligation. We only charge for work once you accept it.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes, completed work comes with a guarantee. We explain the specific scope according to the service before starting.

Which areas do you cover?

We currently serve Barcelona and its metropolitan area. Ask about your municipality and we will confirm availability.

Tell us what you need and we will advise you on the best solution, without obligation.
