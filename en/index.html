Our services

Roller shutter repair, installation and motorisation for homes, commercial premises and offices in Barcelona and nearby areas. Your shutter specialist in Barcelona for fixing faults and improving your shutters.

Repair

Roller shutter repair

We diagnose the fault and fix it the same day whenever possible: straps, strap winders, slats, axles, brackets and motors.

  • Stuck or fallen shutter
  • Broken strap or worn strap winder
  • Damaged slats or slats out of their guide rails
  • Shutter that won't go up or down
  • Axle or bracket problems
  • Faulty motor or electric shutter

Motorisation

Roller shutter motorisation

We automate your shutters so you can control them with a remote, a wall switch or your phone, with no straps or crank handles.

  • Quiet motor installation
  • Remote control or wall switch
  • Control from your phone where compatible
  • Automatic schedules
  • Integration with existing home automation
  • Can be fitted to existing shutters

Frequently asked questions

Can you repair any type of shutter?

We work with aluminium, PVC, security and metal roller shutters, both manual and motorised. If you're not sure, tell us about your case and we'll advise you.

Do you work with aluminium and PVC shutters?

Yes, we install and repair both, as well as security and metal shutters.

Do you install shutters in commercial premises?

Yes, we work in homes as well as commercial premises, shops and offices, including security shutters and metal shutters.

Is there a charge for the visit?

The initial assessment and the quote are free and with no obligation. We only invoice the work once you've accepted it.

Do you offer a guarantee?

Yes, our work comes with a guarantee. Before we start, we explain exactly what it covers for each type of service.
