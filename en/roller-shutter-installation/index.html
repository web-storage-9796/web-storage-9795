Is it time?

When should you replace your roller shutters?

A well-kept shutter lasts many years, but there comes a point when repairing it is no longer worth it. These are the most common signs:

  • The slats are cracked, faded or warped across much of the shutter.
  • Faults keep recurring even after repairs.
  • Cold, heat or noise gets in through the shutter and the shutter box.
  • You're renovating or have just bought a flat and want to replace them all.

Materials

Which roller shutter should you choose?

We advise you during the visit based on the window, which way it faces and what you expect from the shutter. These are the most common options in Barcelona homes:

Aluminium

Strong and durable. With insulating filling, it helps protect your home from the heat in summer and the cold in winter.

PVC

Light and affordable. A good option for small windows and shutters that are raised and lowered by hand.

Reinforced security

Thicker slats and systems that make it harder to lift from outside. Designed for ground floors and top-floor flats.

Before you start

The owners' association and the façade

Roller shutters are part of the building's façade. If you're going to change the colour, material or type of shutter, it's best to check with the owners' association (comunidad de propietarios) first: many buildings require a uniform appearance.

Protected buildings, common in Eixample and Ciutat Vella, may have additional requirements. We help you choose an option that suits the building and know what to ask.

Price

How much does it cost to replace roller shutters?

It mainly depends on the number of windows, their size and the material you choose. That's why we measure first and give you a no-obligation fixed quote. The main factors are:

  • The number and size of the shutters.
  • The material, colour and finish.
  • Whether the guide rails and shutter box can be reused or need renewing.
  • Whether the shutter is manual or motorised.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions about roller shutter installation

How long does an installation take?

A standard installation is usually done in a single visit. For larger projects or several units, we give you an estimated timeframe in the quote.

Is building work needed to replace a shutter?

In many cases, no: if the guide rails and shutter box are in good condition, only the shutter is replaced. If they need renewing, we explain this in the quote.

Do I need the owners' association's permission to replace my shutters?

If you change the exterior appearance (colour, material or type of shutter), it's best to check with the owners' association first, because the façade is a shared element. If you keep the same look, there's usually no problem.

Can I reuse my existing guide rails and shutter box?

Yes, as long as they're in good condition and compatible with the new shutter. We check this during the measuring visit.

Do you work with renovation companies and interior designers?

Yes. We coordinate with whoever is running the renovation to measure and install at the right stage of the works.
